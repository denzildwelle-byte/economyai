Industries we serve

Built for Central Valley businesses

From agriculture to healthcare — we understand the industries that drive Fresno's economy.

Field & crop management

Automated soil monitoring systems can pull data from sensors and trigger irrigation, fertilizer, or pest alerts without anyone checking a dashboard. AI can analyze drone or satellite imagery to flag stressed crops, disease spread, or irrigation gaps before they become expensive problems. Yield prediction models trained on your historical data, weather, and soil conditions can help you plan harvests and contracts more accurately.

Labor & workforce

Scheduling automation is huge in ag — matching crew size to harvest windows, weather forecasts, and field readiness. AI can also handle H-2A compliance paperwork, timesheet collection, and payroll routing, which is notoriously manual and error-prone. Automated onboarding flows for seasonal workers (forms, safety training reminders, document collection) save weeks of admin time each season.

Compliance & food safety

USDA and FDA documentation, pesticide application logs, food safety audit trails, and traceability records can all be auto-generated from field inputs rather than filled in manually. AI can flag when a record is incomplete or a certification is expiring before it becomes a violation.

Sales, contracts & buyer relations

Many growers still manage buyer relationships in spreadsheets. AI can automate contract renewal reminders, produce availability updates to buyers, invoice generation tied to harvest data, and price quote follow-ups. A simple chatbot on a grower's website can handle buyer inquiries 24/7.

Accounting & back office

Automating accounts payable from vendor invoices (OCR + routing for approval), reconciling fuel and equipment costs by field or crop, and generating profit/loss by commodity are all very buildable with current AI tools.

Front desk & patient communication

Appointment scheduling, confirmation calls, and reminders are the single biggest quick win. An AI voice agent or chatbot can handle all inbound scheduling requests, send automated reminders via text/email, and manage cancellation/rebooking — eliminating most of what a front desk person spends their day on.

Insurance & billing

Prior authorization requests, eligibility verification, and claim status follow-ups are brutally manual. AI can automate eligibility checks before appointments, flag likely denial risks on claims before submission, and chase outstanding claims with automated follow-up sequences.

Clinical documentation

AI medical scribes that listen to patient encounters and auto-generate SOAP notes are one of the hottest areas right now. Reducing documentation time by 30–50% is a massive quality-of-life improvement for physicians.

Patient intake & forms

Automating digital intake forms, routing them into the EHR, flagging incomplete information, and triggering pre-visit instructions removes a huge amount of manual data entry.

Referral management

Automating referral tracking — sending the referral, following up with the receiving provider, confirming the patient booked, and closing the loop back to the referring physician — is a workflow most practices desperately need and currently do entirely by phone and fax.

Staff & operations

Shift scheduling for nursing and admin staff, credential expiration tracking, onboarding workflows for new hires, and supply reorder triggers based on usage patterns are all solid mid-tier automation projects.

Lead capture & qualification

An AI chatbot on a brokerage or property management website can engage visitors instantly, ask qualifying questions (budget, timeline, location, buy vs. rent), and either book a showing automatically or route the lead to the right agent. For property managers, the same bot can handle rental inquiries, collect prospect information, and schedule tours — 24/7 without a leasing agent on call.

Follow-up & nurture sequences

Most leads in real estate don't convert for weeks or months. Automated follow-up sequences keep agents top of mind without manual effort. AI can personalize these sequences based on the lead's preferences and flag when a cold lead re-engages so an agent can jump in at the right moment.

Tenant & resident communication

Tenant communication is constant and largely repetitive — maintenance request intake, rent reminders, lease renewal notices, move-in and move-out instructions. All of this can be handled by AI messaging workflows, with escalation to a human only when genuinely needed.

Maintenance coordination

Automating maintenance request triage — collecting the request, categorizing urgency, dispatching the right vendor, confirming scheduling with the tenant, and following up on completion — removes one of the most labor-intensive parts of property management.

Transaction coordination

For brokerages, transaction coordination is a detailed, deadline-heavy process that AI handles well — tracking contingency dates, sending reminders to all parties, collecting signatures, and keeping buyers and sellers informed without an agent managing every touchpoint manually.

Reporting & owner communication

Automated reporting that pulls live data and sends formatted owner summaries on a schedule — along with an AI assistant that can answer owner questions about their portfolio — is a strong mid-tier automation project.
