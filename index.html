Who we serve

Built for Central Valley real estate professionals

We aren't all things to all businesses. Real estate is where we specialize: because the problems are specific, the stakes are high, and generic automation doesn't cut it.

    Lead capture & qualification

    Every lead that lands on your site deserves an immediate response, even at 11 PM on a Saturday. An AI chatbot built for your brokerage or property management office engages visitors the moment they arrive, asks the qualifying questions that matter (budget, timeline, location, buy vs. rent), and either books the showing automatically or routes the lead to the right person. For property managers, the same system handles rental inquiries, collects prospect information, and schedules tours around the clock without a leasing agent on call.

    Follow-up & nurture sequences

    Most real estate leads don't convert for weeks or months, and the agents who close them are the ones who stayed in contact without letting it become a second job. Automated follow-up sequences keep you top of mind at every stage of the lead's journey, personalized to their preferences and timeline. When a cold lead re-engages, you get the flag. The system handled everything in between.

    Tenant & resident communication

    Tenant communication is constant, largely repetitive, and time-consuming: maintenance requests, rent reminders, lease renewal notices, move-in and move-out instructions. AI messaging workflows handle all of it automatically, escalating to a human only when the situation calls for one. Attention goes where it matters most.

    Maintenance coordination

    Maintenance coordination is one of the most labor-intensive parts of property management and one of the easiest to automate well. From the moment a request comes in the system collects the details, categorizes urgency, dispatches the right vendor, confirms scheduling with the tenant, and follows up on completion without anyone manually managing the chain.

    Transaction coordination

    Transaction coordination is deadline-heavy, detail-intensive, and unforgiving when something slips. AI handles it well: tracking contingency dates, sending reminders to all parties, collecting signatures, and keeping buyers, sellers, and lenders informed at every stage without an agent manually managing every touchpoint. Deals move faster. Nothing falls through the cracks.

    Reporting & owner communication

    Owners want to know how their properties are performing without having to ask. Automated reporting pulls live data and delivers formatted portfolio summaries on a schedule you set so owners stay informed and less time is spent fielding status calls. An AI assistant can handle routine owner questions about occupancy, rent collection, and maintenance status around the clock.
