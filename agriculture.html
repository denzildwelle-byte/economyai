The real problem

Why Central Valley agribusinesses
lose money to manual processes

When your margins are tight and your season is short, every hour your team spends on manual work is an hour not spent on the operation.

Harvest season buries your office staff

When volume spikes, so does the paperwork. Weight tickets, lot tracking, field records, and daily reports pile up faster than anyone can process them manually — errors are inevitable.

Compliance documentation is a full-time job

FSMA, GAP, SQF, organic certifications — the recordkeeping requirements never stop. Your team spends hours pulling data and formatting reports that could be generated automatically.

Vendor and buyer communication slips

Following up on purchase orders, confirming delivery windows, chasing invoices — when your team is in the field or on the floor, these fall through the cracks and cost you relationships.

Labor scheduling is chaos at scale

Managing crew availability, shift notifications, time tracking, and payroll data across dozens of workers during peak season is a logistical nightmare without the right systems in place.

Data lives in too many places

Field data in one spreadsheet. Packinghouse records in another. Financials in QuickBooks. Nothing talks to each other. Reconciling it all manually wastes hours every week and creates costly errors.

Knowledge walks out the door

Experienced hands carry critical operational knowledge in their heads. When they leave — or aren't available — that knowledge isn't captured anywhere. Automation creates consistent, documented processes that don't depend on any one person.

What we automate

AI automation services for
Central Valley farms & agribusinesses

Not generic templates. Automations built around the specific workflows of Central Valley growers, packinghouses, and ag operations.

01

Compliance & food safety documentation

AI-powered systems that automatically collect field records, temperature logs, spray applications, and inspection data — then format and file them into your FSMA, GAP, SQF, or organic certification reports. Audit prep that used to take days now takes minutes.

FSMA · GAP · SQF · Organic
02

Harvest & lot tracking automation

Automate the flow of harvest data from field to packinghouse — weight tickets, lot numbers, quality grades, and bin counts captured and logged automatically. Real-time visibility into what's been picked, packed, and shipped without manual data entry at every step.

Harvest · Inventory · Lot tracking
03

Vendor & buyer communication

Automated purchase order confirmations, delivery window reminders, invoice follow-ups, and shipment status updates sent to vendors and buyers without your team having to manually draft and send each one. Relationships stay strong even when your team is at full capacity.

PO management · Invoicing · Logistics
04

Labor scheduling & crew communication

Automated crew scheduling based on field readiness and weather windows, shift notifications sent via text, time and attendance tracking synced to payroll, and daily labor cost reporting — all without a coordinator manually wrangling spreadsheets and phone calls.

Scheduling · Payroll · SMS notifications
05

Field data & crop reporting

Automatically pull irrigation logs, spray records, scouting reports, and yield data from your field systems and consolidate them into formatted daily, weekly, and seasonal reports for ownership, lenders, and buyers — no manual compilation required.

Crop management · Irrigation · Reporting
06

System integration & data consolidation

Connect your farm management software, ERP, accounting system, and spreadsheets so data flows automatically between them. Eliminate double entry, reduce reconciliation time, and get a single accurate picture of your operation without anyone manually transferring data.

FarmLogs · Granular · QuickBooks · ERP
07

Customer & broker relationship management

Automated availability updates sent to your buyer network when new product comes in, price list distributions, seasonal outreach campaigns, and follow-up sequences for inquiries — keeping your relationships warm without your sales team manually reaching out to every contact.

CRM · Email · Broker communication
