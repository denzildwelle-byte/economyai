01

Instant lead response & qualification

Every new inquiry — Zillow, your website, a Google ad, a sign call — gets a personalized text or email response within 60 seconds, around the clock. The AI asks the right qualifying questions: timeline, budget, buy vs. rent, preferred neighborhoods. It books a showing or consultation directly on your calendar and sends you a full qualification summary so when you step out of your showing, the lead is already warm and waiting — not cold and gone to a competitor who was faster.

Works with: Zillow leads, website forms, Google Ads leads, sign call services, Realtor.com inquiries, Facebook lead ads.

Lead capture · Qualification · Auto-booking
02

Automated follow-up & nurture sequences

AI-powered drip sequences that stay personal — triggered by where each lead is in their journey, their property preferences, and how they're engaging with your content. When a lead who went quiet six weeks ago re-opens your email or clicks a listing, you get an immediate alert so you can call at exactly the right moment. Your entire pipeline gets consistent, personalized follow-up without you drafting a single message — across email, text, and voicemail drop.

Works with: Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, BoomTown, LionDesk, Chime, HubSpot. Supports email, SMS, and voicemail drop channels.

Email · SMS · Behavioral triggers · Voicemail drop
03

CRM updates & transaction coordination

Every call, showing, email, and deal stage change automatically logged and synced to your CRM — no more 40-minute daily maintenance sessions. From accepted offer to close, automated checklists track every deadline: inspection periods, contingency removals, loan commitment dates, closing disclosures. Document requests go out automatically. Buyers and sellers get status update messages at every milestone — work started, phase complete, inspection scheduled — without you drafting each one. Your clients feel informed and taken care of. Your CRM stays clean and current.

Works with: DocuSign, Dotloop, SkySlope, Glide, most transaction management platforms and CRMs.

CRM sync · Document management · Transaction tracking · Client comms
04

Past client & referral nurture

Your past clients are your best source of new business — but staying in meaningful, consistent contact with hundreds of people across multiple years is impossible to do manually at scale. Automated closing anniversary messages, personalized local market updates, and life-event-triggered outreach (new job, marriage, growing family indicators) keep you top of mind year-round with zero manual effort. When a past client is ready to move again or refers a friend, they think of you first because you never really went quiet — even years later.

Works with: your CRM's past client database, email, SMS. Can pull live market data to personalize update content for each client's neighbourhood.

Past clients · Referral generation · Market updates · Anniversary outreach
