The real problem

Why Central Valley contractors
lose bids and revenue to slow processes

You're running crews, managing subs, and keeping jobs on schedule. The last thing you need is to be buried in paperwork, playing phone tag with leads, and chasing invoices after hours.

Leads go cold while you're on the job site

A homeowner or GC submits an inquiry at 2pm. You're on a roof in Clovis. By the time you call back at 6pm they've already booked your competitor who answered within the hour. Speed to response wins bids in construction.

Estimates take hours that should take minutes

Every proposal built from scratch — pulling line items, calculating labor, pricing materials, formatting the document. A multi-hour process for work that's largely templated. That time should be spent on the job site or with the next prospect.

Crew scheduling is managed by text message

Coordinating who goes where, notifying crew of schedule changes, managing callouts and reroutings — all done manually via group texts and phone calls. When a job runs long or someone calls out sick, the ripple effect takes hours to sort out.

Invoices go out late and collections drag

Between managing active jobs and chasing new work, invoicing gets pushed to evenings and weekends. Payment reminders are sent inconsistently. Overdue balances pile up. Cash flow suffers because the billing process isn't systematic.

Safety and compliance docs are a scramble

OSHA safety checklists, certification tracking, incident documentation, and compliance audit trails managed in paper binders or random shared folders. When an inspector shows up or a claim is filed, finding the right documentation becomes a crisis.

Job updates and client communication fall behind

Clients want to know what's happening on their project. Without an automated update system, communication depends on whoever has a free moment — which is rarely anyone during a busy week. Silence breeds anxiety and disputes.

What we automate

AI automation services for
Central Valley construction & trades

Not generic templates. Automations built around the specific workflows of Central Valley general contractors, specialty trades, and home service companies.

01

Lead response & qualification

An AI chatbot or voice agent responds to inbound inquiries instantly — day or night — asking the right qualifying questions (project type, timeline, location, budget range) and either booking an estimate appointment automatically or routing the lead to the right person on your team. No lead sits unanswered while you're on site. Speed to response is one of the biggest factors in winning residential and commercial bids.

Lead capture · Qualification · Scheduling
02

Estimate & proposal generation

AI pulls from your library of standard line items, labor rates, and material costs to generate a formatted proposal from a project intake form or site visit notes — turning a multi-hour estimating process into a quick review and approval. Proposals go out faster, look more professional, and follow up automatically when a prospect goes quiet. You control every number before it goes to the client.

Estimating · Proposals · Follow-up
03

Job scheduling & crew dispatch

AI scheduling tools optimize crew assignments across active jobs based on availability, skill set, location, and job requirements — automatically notifying technicians of their daily schedule, sending reminders before each job, and dynamically rerouting when a job runs long or a crew member calls out. What used to require a coordinator managing texts and calls all morning runs automatically before the workday starts.

Scheduling · Dispatch · SMS notifications
04

Invoicing, payments & collections

AI automates invoice generation tied to project milestones or completed work orders, sends payment reminders on a consistent schedule, flags overdue invoices for follow-up, and processes payment confirmations automatically. Invoices go out the day work is completed — not a week later when you get a chance. Collections become systematic instead of something you have to remember to chase.

Invoicing · Payment reminders · Collections
05

Safety & compliance documentation

AI automates safety checklist completion from mobile devices in the field — no more paper binders or chasing crew to fill out forms at the end of the day. Certification and training expiration dates tracked automatically for every crew member with advance alerts before anything lapses. A complete, searchable compliance audit trail that's always current and accessible when an inspector shows up or a claim needs to be documented.

OSHA · Safety checklists · Certifications
06

Client communication & project updates

Automated project status updates sent to clients at key milestones — work started, phase completed, inspection scheduled, final walkthrough booked — without anyone on your team manually drafting and sending each message. Clients feel informed and confident in the project without calling to ask what's going on. Disputes and scope creep conversations happen less often when communication is consistent.

Client updates · Milestones · Communication
07

Subcontractor & vendor management

Automated sub and vendor communication — scope confirmation, schedule reminders, document requests, insurance certificate tracking, and payment processing — all managed systematically rather than by whoever remembers to follow up. Insurance and license expiration dates flagged automatically before they create liability exposure on an active job site.

Subcontractors · Vendors · Insurance tracking
