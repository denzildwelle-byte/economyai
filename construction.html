What we automate

Four automations that pay for themselves
in the first month.

Built around the workflows of Central Valley general contractors and specialty trades. We integrate with Buildertrend, CoConstruct, Procore, JobNimbus, ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, and QuickBooks.

01

Instant lead response

Every inquiry — website form, Google ad, Angi lead — gets a personalized text or call back within 60 seconds, around the clock. The AI qualifies the lead (project type, timeline, budget), books an estimate appointment on your calendar, and only pings you when a real opportunity is locked in.

Lead capture · Qualification · Booking
02

Estimate & proposal generation

From site visit notes or an intake form, AI builds a formatted proposal pulling from your library of line items, labor rates, and material costs. Hours of work becomes a quick review. Proposals follow up automatically when a prospect goes quiet. You approve every number before it goes out.

Estimating · Proposals · Auto follow-up
03

Invoicing & collections

Invoices generate the day work is complete, tied to project milestones. Payment reminders run on a consistent schedule without anyone having to remember. Overdue accounts flag for personal follow-up. Cash flow becomes a system instead of a scramble.

Invoicing · Reminders · Collections
04

Crew scheduling & client updates

Daily schedules sent to crew via text before the workday starts. Reroutes happen automatically when a job runs long or someone calls out. Clients get milestone update messages — work started, phase complete, inspection scheduled — without anyone drafting them.

Dispatch · SMS · Client updates
