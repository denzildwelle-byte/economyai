The real problem

Why Central Valley real estate agents
lose deals to manual processes

The Central Valley market moves fast. Manual processes are costing you deals you don't even know you're losing.

Leads go cold while you're on a showing

A new inquiry hits your inbox at 2pm. You're with a client. By the time you follow up, they've already booked a showing with the agent who responded in 4 minutes.

Your CRM is always out of date

Deal stages, contact notes, follow-up dates — manually updating all of it takes 30–45 minutes a day that should be spent building relationships.

Follow-up sequences fall apart

Most real estate leads take 60–180 days to convert. Keeping up a consistent, personalized nurture sequence manually is nearly impossible without dropping the ball.

Admin work owns your evenings

Preparing CMAs, chasing signatures, sending transaction updates, coordinating inspections — the paperwork never stops, and most of it doesn't need a human to do it.

Missed calls mean missed commissions

When a buyer or seller can't reach you immediately, they don't wait. They move to the next agent. There's no recovery from a missed first contact in a hot market.

Referrals slip through the cracks

Past clients are your best source of business. But staying in consistent, meaningful touch with a growing database of hundreds of people is impossible without automation.

What we automate

AI automation services for
Central Valley real estate agents

Not generic templates. Automations designed around the specific workflows of Central Valley agents and brokerages.

01

Instant lead response & qualification

An AI assistant responds to every new inquiry within minutes — day or night — asks the right qualifying questions (timeline, budget, buy vs. rent, location), and either books a showing automatically or routes the lead to you with a full qualification summary. No lead goes cold while you're with a client.

Lead capture · CRM · Chatbot
02

Automated follow-up & nurture sequences

AI-powered drip sequences that stay personal — triggered by where each lead is in their journey, their property preferences, and their engagement behavior. When a cold lead re-engages, you get an immediate alert so you can jump in at exactly the right moment.

Email · SMS · CRM automation
03

CRM updates & pipeline management

Every call, showing, email, and status change automatically logged and synced to your CRM. Deal stages update based on activity. Stale deals get flagged. You open your CRM and it's actually accurate — without spending 40 minutes a day maintaining it.

CRM · Workflow automation
04

Transaction coordination automation

From accepted offer to close — automated checklists, deadline reminders to all parties, document collection workflows, and status update emails to buyers and sellers. Your clients feel informed and cared for without you sending the same email six times per transaction.

Document management · Comms
05

Listing prep & market reports

AI can pull comparable sales, generate formatted CMA drafts, and produce personalized market update emails for your entire database on a scheduled basis — keeping you top of mind with past clients and prospects without any manual research.

Reporting · Client communication
06

Past client & referral nurture

Automated anniversary emails, market updates, and check-in sequences for your entire past client database. Triggers for life events like move-in anniversaries, rate drops, or local market shifts that make your outreach feel timely and relevant — not like a mass blast.

Email · Referral pipeline
07

Review & reputation management

Automated post-close review request sequences sent at the right moment via text and email. AI monitors new reviews across Google and Zillow, drafts personalized responses for your approval, and flags anything negative for immediate attention before it does damage.

Reviews · Reputation · Google
