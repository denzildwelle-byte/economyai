Common questions

Pricing questions answered

Do I need a retainer if I buy a one-time project?

No. Every delivery tier includes post-launch support (30–90 days depending on tier). The retainer is optional for businesses that want ongoing optimization, new features, or peace of mind that someone's watching the system.

What if my project doesn't fit a tier?

The tiers cover 90% of requests, but if your needs fall outside them, we'll scope a custom quote. Book a free audit and we'll walk through exactly what you need and what it'll cost.

Can I upgrade tiers mid-project?

Yes. If we discover during scoping that you need more than initially planned, we'll show you the cost difference and let you decide whether to expand scope or stick with the original plan.

What happens after the included support period ends?

Your automation keeps running — it's yours. If you want ongoing monitoring, updates, or expansion, you can add a retainer. If you just need occasional help, we offer one-off support at $175/hour.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. For Professional and Enterprise tiers, we offer 50% upfront and 50% on delivery. For larger engagements, we can structure milestone-based payments. Just ask during the audit call.

What if the automation doesn't work as expected?

We guarantee functionality. If something doesn't work as scoped, we fix it at no charge during the support period. If requirements change after launch, we treat it as new development (billable or covered by retainer).
