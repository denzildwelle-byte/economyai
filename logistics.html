The real problem

Why Central Valley logistics companies
lose margin to manual processes

In logistics, every manual step is a cost. Every delayed notification is a customer service call. Every suboptimal route is margin left on the road.

Customers call for updates you should be sending

Inbound "where's my shipment?" calls flood your customer service team because proactive notifications aren't going out automatically at each status change. Your team spends hours answering questions that a well-built notification system would have answered before the customer even thought to ask.

Dispatch planning takes half the morning

Route planning done manually — balancing delivery windows, driver availability, vehicle capacity, HOS regulations, and fuel costs — is a multi-hour process that has to be repeated every single morning. That's not a planning problem. That's an automation problem.

Carrier selection leaves money on the table

Manually comparing rates, calling reps, and booking loads across a carrier panel takes time your team doesn't have and consistently produces results that are worse than what automated freight procurement delivers. Companies moving significant volume are leaving 8–15% in freight savings unrealized.

Exceptions turn into customer crises

A delay, a damaged shipment, a missed delivery window — when these are caught manually, the customer finds out before you do. By the time your team is working the exception, the customer is already frustrated and your service reputation is taking the hit.

HOS and compliance reporting is a constant burden

Driver log monitoring, HOS violation flags, vehicle inspection tracking, and recurring regulatory reports managed manually are a significant admin burden — and a liability risk when something gets missed before an audit or a citation.

Claims take weeks to close

When a claim is filed, collecting documentation from drivers, warehouse staff, and carriers — and routing it all to the right people with consistent follow-up — is a manual, time-consuming process that drags resolution timelines and damages relationships while it sits open.

What we automate

AI automation services for
Central Valley logistics & distribution

Not generic templates. Automations built around the specific workflows of Central Valley carriers, brokers, and distribution operations.

01

Shipment tracking & customer notifications

An AI-powered tracking portal combined with automated proactive notifications — triggered at every status change from pickup through final delivery — eliminates the bulk of inbound customer service calls before they happen. When exceptions occur, AI detects them automatically and notifies the customer with a revised ETA before they even know something went wrong. Customers feel informed. Your CS team handles genuine issues instead of status queries.

Tracking · Proactive notifications · Customer updates
02

Route optimization & dispatch

AI route optimization accounts for delivery windows, vehicle capacity, driver hours-of-service regulations, traffic patterns, and fuel costs — consistently reducing total miles driven and increasing stops per route. Automated dispatch workflows turn a half-day planning process into a fully automated morning operation. Drivers get their optimized route on their phone before they leave the yard, and dynamic rerouting happens in real time when conditions change mid-day.

Route optimization · Dispatch · Dynamic rerouting
03

Carrier management & freight procurement

AI automates carrier selection across a pre-approved panel, compares spot rates in real time, generates booking confirmations, tracks carrier performance over time, and flags underperforming carriers before they become a service problem. For companies moving significant freight volume, automated freight procurement consistently delivers 8–15% cost reductions without sacrificing service levels — because decisions are made on data, not relationships and gut feel.

Carrier selection · Spot rates · Performance tracking
04

Exception management & claims

AI identifies shipment exceptions in real time — delays, damages, missed windows, refused deliveries — automatically opens a claim or exception workflow, collects required documentation from drivers and warehouse staff, and routes everything to the right person for resolution with automated follow-up until it closes. Resolution timelines compress significantly. Customers are notified proactively at every step rather than chasing updates.

Exception detection · Claims · Automated resolution
05

Compliance & regulatory reporting

AI automates driver log monitoring, flags HOS violations before they become citations, tracks vehicle inspection schedules and maintenance intervals, and generates the recurring regulatory reports required for DOT compliance — all without a compliance coordinator manually pulling data and building reports every week. Audit prep that used to take days now takes minutes.

HOS monitoring · DOT compliance · Inspection tracking
06

Warehouse & inventory automation

AI-powered receiving workflows that capture inbound shipments, match them against purchase orders, flag discrepancies automatically, and update inventory in real time — without manual data entry at every step. Automated low-stock alerts and replenishment triggers prevent stockouts before they disrupt outbound operations. Pick-and-pack workflow optimization reduces warehouse labor costs without reducing throughput.

Receiving · Inventory · Replenishment · Warehouse ops
07

Billing, invoicing & collections

Automated freight invoice generation from completed load data, rate confirmation matching against carrier invoices to flag billing discrepancies before payment, payment reminder sequences for outstanding receivables, and accessorial charge documentation collected automatically from drivers in the field. Cash flow improves when billing is systematic rather than dependent on someone finding time to reconcile loads at the end of the week.

Freight billing · Invoice matching · Collections
