The real problem

Why Central Valley law firms
lose revenue to non-billable work

Every hour an attorney spends on intake forms, document drafting, deadline chasing, and billing reconciliation is an hour not billed to a client. That math compounds fast.

Client intake consumes too much attorney time

Sending questionnaires, chasing missing information, running conflict checks, collecting engagement letters, and populating the case management system manually before a matter even opens is a significant drain on attorney and paralegal time.

Routine documents take hours they shouldn't

NDAs, engagement letters, demand letters, standard contracts, and operating agreements are drafted from scratch each time — even when 80% of the content is identical. That's billable time that should be spent on substantive legal work.

Deadlines fall through the cracks

Critical filing deadlines, statute of limitations dates, and court appearance reminders managed in a shared calendar or a paralegal's spreadsheet are a malpractice risk waiting to happen. Manual tracking is unreliable at scale.

Billable time goes uncaptured

Attorneys are notoriously poor at capturing time in the moment. End-of-day or end-of-week reconstruction from memory means revenue walks out the door every day. The average firm loses 30–40% of billable time to undercapture.

Invoicing and collections are manual and slow

Generating invoices, sending them, following up on overdue balances, reconciling trust accounts, and managing payment plans are all handled manually — consuming bookkeeper and attorney time that should be automated.

Regulatory changes catch clients off guard

For employment law, HR consulting, and compliance-focused practices, clients need to know about regulatory changes quickly. Manually monitoring feeds and drafting client alerts is time-consuming and inconsistent.

What we automate

AI automation services for
Central Valley law firms

Not generic templates. Automations built around the specific workflows of Central Valley legal practices — from solo attorneys to multi-practice firms.

01

Client intake & onboarding

AI automates the entire intake process — sending digital questionnaires to prospective clients, collecting signed engagement letters, running automated conflict checks against your existing client database, and populating your case management system before the attorney ever speaks to the client. New matters open faster, with complete information, and with zero manual data entry.

Intake · Conflict checks · Case management
02

Document drafting & assembly

AI generates first drafts of standard legal documents — NDAs, engagement letters, demand letters, contracts, operating agreements, and settlement agreements — from a structured intake form or matter data. Drafting time drops from two hours to fifteen minutes on routine documents, with attorneys reviewing and approving every document before it goes out. Nothing leaves the firm without human sign-off.

Document drafting · NDAs · Contracts
03

Deadline & calendar management

AI monitors all active matters and tracks critical deadlines — filing dates, statute of limitations, court appearances, discovery cutoffs, and contract milestones — sending escalating reminders to responsible attorneys well in advance. Anything at risk of slipping is flagged automatically, far more reliably than a shared calendar or paralegal-managed spreadsheet.

Deadlines · Matter tracking · Reminders
04

Billing & time capture

AI reconstructs billable time from calendar entries, emails, document activity, and phone logs — suggesting time entries for attorney review rather than requiring manual logging from memory. Automated invoice generation, payment reminder sequences, trust account reconciliation, and overdue balance follow-up all run without bookkeeper involvement on routine matters. Leakage drops significantly.

Time capture · Invoicing · Collections
05

Compliance & regulatory monitoring

AI monitors regulatory feeds, flags relevant rule changes in your practice areas, and generates formatted client alert drafts automatically — essential for employment law, HR consultancies, accounting firms, and compliance-focused practices whose clients need to know when something changes before it affects them. Alerts go out faster and more consistently than any manual monitoring process.

Regulatory monitoring · Client alerts · Compliance
06

Client communication & matter updates

Automated status update messages sent to clients at key matter milestones — filings submitted, documents received, hearings scheduled, and settlements reached — without an attorney or paralegal drafting and sending each one manually. Clients feel informed and cared for. Inbound "what's the status?" calls drop significantly, freeing your staff for substantive work.

Client updates · Matter milestones · Communication
07

Lead intake & consultation scheduling

An AI chatbot or voice agent on your firm's website engages prospective clients around the clock, collects initial matter details, screens for conflicts, and books consultation appointments automatically — so your intake coordinator isn't the bottleneck between a potential client and their first conversation with an attorney.

Lead intake · Scheduling · Chatbot
