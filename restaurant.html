The real problem

Why Central Valley restaurants
lose revenue to operational friction

You're in the business of hospitality — but between no-shows, scheduling chaos, inventory waste, and reputation management, you're spending half your time running a spreadsheet operation instead of a restaurant.

No-shows cost you covers you can't get back

A party of six that doesn't show up is six covers gone, a table sat empty during your busiest hour, and a waitlist full of guests who could have filled it — if you'd released the table in time. Manual reminder systems are inconsistent and easy to forget during a rush.

Negative reviews sit unanswered for days

A one-star review on Google or Yelp that goes unresponded for a week does real damage. Manually monitoring every platform and drafting thoughtful, personalized responses for every review — positive and negative — is time your management team doesn't have during service.

Scheduling is a weekly nightmare

Building a schedule that matches labor to actual anticipated demand — accounting for historical sales, day-of-week patterns, events, and weather — manually in a spreadsheet consistently produces either overstaffed slow nights or understaffed Friday rushes. Both hurt your bottom line.

Food cost variance bleeds profit quietly

The gap between your theoretical food cost and your actual food cost grows every week that inventory counts, waste tracking, and purchase orders aren't systematic. Most restaurant operators know the number is wrong — they just don't have time to find where it's leaking.

Event inquiries get lost in the shuffle

A private dining inquiry that sits in your email for two days while service is happening is a lost booking. The follow-up, proposal, contract, deposit, and event brief process managed manually means some events get great attention and others fall through due to bandwidth.

Reservation management ties up your host

Answering reservation calls, managing modifications, maintaining the waitlist, and releasing held tables manually keeps your host on the phone instead of on the floor welcoming guests — the job they're actually there to do.

What we automate

AI automation services for
Central Valley restaurants & hospitality

Not generic templates. Automations built around the specific workflows of Central Valley restaurants, bars, catering operations, and hospitality businesses.

01

Reservations & waitlist management

An AI voice agent or chatbot handles reservation intake and modifications around the clock — so guests can book, cancel, or reschedule at any hour without calling the host stand. Automated confirmation and reminder messages go out at 48 hours and again 2 hours before every reservation, dramatically reducing no-show rates. When a confirmed party fails to arrive within a set window, the table is automatically released and offered to the next guest on the waitlist via SMS.

Reservations · No-show reduction · Waitlist · SMS
02

Customer communication & reputation management

AI monitors every new review across Google, Yelp, TripAdvisor, and OpenTable in real time, drafts personalized responses for owner or manager approval, and flags urgent negative reviews immediately — before they sit unanswered for days. Automated post-visit messages requesting a review are sent at the right moment after the dining experience and consistently lift review volume and average rating by 3× or more compared to manually asking.

Review monitoring · Response drafts · Post-visit requests
03

Staff scheduling & labor management

AI scheduling tools forecast covers by shift using historical sales data, weather, local events, and day-of-week patterns — then generate an optimized schedule that matches labor to anticipated demand. Overstaffing on slow nights and understaffing on busy ones both cost you money. Automated schedule publishing, shift reminders, and swap management reduce the back-and-forth that consumes managers every week. The right people, at the right hours, at the right cost.

Labor forecasting · Schedule optimization · Shift management
04

Inventory & food cost management

AI automates inventory tracking from POS sales data, generates purchase orders based on projected need and par levels, flags variance between theoretical and actual food cost by category, and identifies waste patterns by day, shift, or menu item. The gap between what your food cost should be and what it actually is gets smaller every week that the system is running — and you stop finding out about problems at month-end when it's too late to fix them.

Inventory · POs · Food cost · Variance tracking
05

Event & catering coordination

AI automates the entire event inquiry workflow — capturing requirements from an inquiry form, generating a customized proposal, routing the contract for e-signature, collecting the deposit, sending pre-event questionnaires, and distributing a structured event brief to your kitchen and front-of-house team in advance. Private dining, buyouts, and catering jobs get the same systematic attention whether you have one event that week or ten.

Event intake · Proposals · Contracts · Event briefs
06

Guest loyalty & re-engagement

Automated guest re-engagement sequences triggered by dining history — birthday and anniversary messages with a personalized offer, lapsed guest win-back campaigns for guests who haven't visited in 60 days, and VIP recognition messages for your highest-frequency regulars. Your most valuable guests feel remembered and appreciated without anyone on your team manually tracking who's been in and when.

Loyalty · Birthday campaigns · Guest retention
07

Online ordering & delivery integration

Automated order confirmation and status updates across your delivery platforms — DoorDash, Uber Eats, your own online ordering — with order routing to the right kitchen station, prep time optimization based on current ticket load, and automated customer communication when items are unavailable or orders are running behind. Delivery operations that run more smoothly and generate fewer one-star reviews.

Online ordering · Delivery · Order management
