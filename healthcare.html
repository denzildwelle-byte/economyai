The real problem

Why Central Valley medical practices
lose hours to administrative work

Physicians didn't go to medical school to spend half their day on documentation, insurance calls, and scheduling logistics. Neither did your staff.

Physicians are buried in documentation

The average physician spends 2 hours on EHR documentation for every 1 hour of patient care. That's time away from patients, and a primary driver of burnout among Central Valley providers.

Front desk is overwhelmed with scheduling

Appointment requests, confirmations, reminders, cancellations, and rescheduling consume the majority of your front desk team's day — leaving little bandwidth for the patients standing in front of them.

Insurance and billing is brutally manual

Prior authorizations, eligibility verification, claim status follow-ups, and denial management require hours of phone calls and data entry that your billing staff shouldn't have to do manually.

Referrals fall through the cracks

Sending a referral is one thing. Following up to confirm the patient booked, ensure records were received, and close the loop back to the referring provider is a labor-intensive process most practices manage entirely by phone and fax.

Patient intake is still paper-heavy

Manually processing intake forms, re-entering data into the EHR, chasing incomplete information, and routing pre-visit instructions are all tasks that can be fully automated — but most practices still do them by hand.

Staff management is a constant burden

Scheduling nursing and admin staff, tracking credential expiration dates, managing onboarding paperwork for new hires, and handling supply reorders consume hours of management time that should be spent on patient care strategy.

What we automate

AI automation services for
Central Valley medical practices

Not generic templates. Automations built around the specific workflows of Central Valley healthcare providers — from solo practices to multi-location groups.

01

Front desk & patient scheduling

An AI voice agent or chatbot handles all inbound scheduling requests, sends automated appointment confirmations and reminders via text and email, manages cancellations and rescheduling, and follows up on no-shows automatically. Eliminates the majority of what your front desk staff spends their day on — letting them focus on the patients in front of them.

Scheduling · SMS reminders · Voice agent
02

Insurance verification & prior authorization

AI automates eligibility verification before every appointment, flags coverage issues before the patient arrives, submits prior authorization requests, monitors approval status, and follows up with payers automatically. Denial risks are flagged on claims before submission — not after they bounce. Cuts one of the most time-consuming and frustrating parts of your billing workflow significantly.

Eligibility · Prior auth · Claims
03

Clinical documentation & AI scribing

AI medical scribes listen to patient encounters and auto-generate structured SOAP notes, reducing physician documentation time by 30–50%. Notes are ready for physician review immediately after the encounter — not at the end of a 12-hour day. One of the highest-ROI interventions for physician well-being and practice throughput available today.

SOAP notes · EHR · Documentation
04

Patient intake & digital forms

Automated digital intake packets sent to patients before their appointment, with intelligent routing into the EHR once completed. Incomplete forms are flagged and followed up automatically. Pre-visit instructions, consent forms, and health history questionnaires all processed without a single manual data entry step.

Digital intake · EHR integration · Forms
05

Referral management & coordination

Automated referral tracking that sends the referral, follows up with the receiving provider, confirms the patient booked their appointment, ensures records were received, and closes the loop back to the referring physician — all without a coordinator managing it by phone and fax. Reduces referral leakage and keeps your care relationships intact.

Referrals · Care coordination · Follow-up
06

Staff scheduling & operations

AI-optimized shift scheduling for nursing and admin staff based on patient volume forecasts, automated credential expiration tracking with advance alerts, structured onboarding workflows for new hires, and supply reorder triggers based on usage patterns — all without a manager manually tracking everything in spreadsheets.

Scheduling · Credentialing · Onboarding
07

Patient communication & recall

Automated preventive care recall sequences — annual wellness visits, follow-up appointments, lab result notifications, and chronic disease management check-ins — sent via text and email on a schedule without anyone manually pulling lists and reaching out. Keeps your patient panel engaged and improves outcomes without adding headcount.

Recall · Preventive care · Patient engagement
